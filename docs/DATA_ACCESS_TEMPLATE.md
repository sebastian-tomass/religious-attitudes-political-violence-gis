# Data and Methodology

The dataset including interview answers and questions was taken from the Arab Barometer Wave VII survey conducted across 12 Arab countries in 2021 and 2022 (Arab Barometer, 2022).

Data on political violence comes from ACLED, which tracks geolocated conflict incidents involving both state and non-state actors (Armed Conflict Location & Event Data (ACLED), n.d.).

Administrative boundaries are sourced from geoBoundaries and supplementary humanitarian files (Runfola et al., 2020).
